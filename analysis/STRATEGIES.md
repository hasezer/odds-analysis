## Lessons: what did NOT work (do not redo without a new idea)

- No strategy beats Nesine's prices in general: ML on all markets, 21M mined patterns, sure-bet scans, rule stacking and 'price + corrections' all lost money on unseen seasons (reports: ML_REPORT, MINER_REPORT, RULES_V3_REPORT). Only exception so far: Portugal draw with 3+ goals (promising).
- For hit rate on high-odds bets, the bet's own odds are the best filter (top 1% = 2.8x normal). Machine learning, rule counts and 'odds + corrections' were all worse on unseen seasons (HIGH_ODDS_REPORT).
- Rules raise hit rates 1.5-3x, but the odds fall with them; only ~17% of readable rules beat a plain odds filter of the same size, and confirmed rules do not hit more than the odds of their own matches predict.
- Team-level 'beats the price' effects do not persist from season to season (same count as shuffled data).
- League tendencies that beat the normal rate and the goal model every season still LOSE 10-25% at Nesine's real odds (Argentina draws, MLS away 3+, Bundesliga goals...). Only Portugal draw with 3+ goals made money every season; Ligue 1 half-time 'away ahead & both scored' is near break-even (+19.6 / -2.5 / -2.7).
- Rule hunt v4 (money scans, every bet type x odds band, Nesine's cut, league, weekday, kick-off hour, month, season stage, rest days, league x odds): nothing made money in all three seasons except Portugal draws with 3+ goals (strongest at odds > 13.95) and a weak La Liga 1-1 slice; shuffled labels give ~0 (RULES_V4_REPORT).
- Portugal 'draw & over 2.5' is really a Portugal 2-2 effect: 53 of its 62 wins were 2-2 (8 were 3-3), 22 teams, every part of the season, every odds band >= 0.95. Portugal draws are high-scoring for its goal rate (2-2 is 75% as common as 1-1; Spain 28%, Argentina 18%) and Nesine prices X & under 2.5 there at 0.51-0.64.
- Rule hunt v5 (new information): corners from team corner/cross/shot stats predict exactly as well as Nesine's line (error 2.68 vs 2.68) and corner bets need 61-70% to break even (lose 8-18%); teams' first/second-half habits do not persist (correlation ~0); late-season motivation from rebuilt league tables shows no steady effect; popular teams (Turkey big 4, global giants) are NOT over-priced - their opponents lose 41-56%; a money scan of 915 non-odds conditions x 162 bet types (~148,000 cells) kept only Portugal draw with 3+ goals (shuffled: 0) (RULES_V5_REPORT).
- Every bet type loses over time at Nesine's closing odds: the least bad lose ~11-15 per 100 (both score in both halves, 1st-half 1-1, BTTS yes, 2nd-half BTTS, low totals); correct scores lose ~47 on average and capped long shots 55-93; HT/FT ~26; corners ~18 (output/bet_type_returns.xlsx).
- Owner's tipster list (16 Oct 2026, tipster_rules.py, output/tipster_rules.xlsx): 21 of 22 rules testable; many raise the hit rate (e.g. 0-1 goals odds > 4.20 -> over 3.5: 42% vs 30%; HT score 'other' < 5 & score 'other' < 8 -> 6+ goals: 23% vs 6%) but every rule stays below break-even (-8 to -33 per 100); 'all three half-by-half BTTS bets every game' loses 16%; the only plus (#21, HT/FT 2/1, +88) is 2 wins in 34 matches.
- Tipster rule 5 by league (tipster_rule5.py, output/rule5_by_league.xlsx/.png): a higher 'total goals 0-1' price raises 6+ goals in every league (all leagues: 3.6% at <= 3.50 -> 19-23% above 7.00), but no league x odds range made money in all three seasons (0 cells; shuffled leagues 0.6). Closest: Switzerland, 0-1 > 5.00: 19% vs break-even 18% (-0.4 per 100, 126 bets).
- 6+ goals deep dive (SIX_PLUS_REPORT): Nesine's 6+ price is the best 6+ signal; 555 signals, structure ratios, a small combined model and 5,168 confirmed threshold rules add nothing beyond it (1 rule beat the cheapest-6+ filter of the same size).

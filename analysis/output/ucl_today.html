What a pick is

Each pick is a rule from our rule book that fires on this match's current Nesine odds. A rule is up to three price conditions, for example "Draw 3.05–3.15 and away team under 0.5 at 3.34–4.00". Every rule was found in 2024/25, confirmed in 2025/26 and checked again in 2026/27 + 2026, on 20,000+ matches collected by this project.

The numbers on a pick

Expected hit starts from the chance Nesine's price gives the bet today (cut removed) and adds half of the edge the rule had over Nesine's price in the past, using the careful rate (the lower of the rule's all-seasons and latest-season hit rates). Half, because on matches the rules had never seen, their edge shrank. A rule only counts when today's price looks like the prices it was learned on. Normally is how often the bet lands in an average match. Value is expected hit × odds − 1: what 100 TL would return on average if the estimate holds.

Read this before betting

The rules really raise hit rates on matches they have never seen. But Nesine's price usually rises with them, so most picks show negative value: you would still lose, just less than betting blindly. Picks marked above price are where the rule's hit rate beats Nesine's price today. History says treat those as a watch list, not a sure thing. Samples behind each rule are a few hundred matches.

Reliability

We repeated the search with shuffled results to see how many rules pure luck would produce. very reliable means luck explains under 2% of a bet's rules, reliable under 10%, mixed under 50%. Bets where most rules could be luck are left out.

Caps

Prices struck through in red sit at Nesine's maximum (correct score 120+, HT/FT and combos 34+). At the cap the outcome is far less likely than the price suggests: these lost 40–95% in every season.
